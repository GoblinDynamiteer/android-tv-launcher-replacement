Hue scenes on the home screen

Three takes on the top-left corner, mirroring the input bar on the right. The rooms, zones and scenes are your real ones from the bridge (Lounge Colors, Vardagsrum, …), and each scene's dot shows its actual colours. Click a frame, then use the arrow keys and Enter. Esc closes a panel.

H1 · Current scene, list on OK

The bar only shows what's on now ("Rolling hills" in Lounge Colors). OK drops a glass list of that zone's scenes below it; ◀ ▶ switches room or zone, OK sets the scene. The list ends with Lights off. Quietest on the home screen.

H2 · Favourite scenes in the bar

Three or four favourites straight in the bar, like the inputs: OK sets one at once, a small dot marks the active scene. ··· opens the full list from H1. Fastest for the scenes you use every evening.

H3 · Scene cards

A light-bulb button; OK opens a wide glass panel with the scenes as colour cards (like the Hue app), tabs for each room or zone. Most visual, and shows the palettes best.
