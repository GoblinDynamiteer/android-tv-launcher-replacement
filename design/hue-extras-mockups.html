Hue: brightness and favourites

Additions to the Hue corner (H1, as built). Your real rooms, zones, scenes and colours. Click a frame, then use the arrow keys. Enter = OK, hold Enter (or press F) to star or unstar a scene, Esc = Back. Relax and Movie Night start as favourites.

B · Brightness slider in the list

A slider sits under the room name. On it, ◀ ▶ change the brightness in 10 % steps; the value is sent once you pause, so holding the key doesn't flood the bridge. On a scene, ◀ ▶ still switch room. Greyed out while the room is off; turning it up switches it on. A star marks favourites.

F1 · Favourites in the bar

Starred scenes become one-press buttons next to the current scene. OK on a favourite sets it at once; OK on the current scene opens the list (with the slider) as before. The bar grows by one segment per favourite, so it works best with two or three.

F2 · Favourites at the top of the list

The bar stays as it is. The list starts with a Favourites section across all rooms (with the room's name after each), then the room header, slider and its scenes. Two presses for a favourite, but the home screen stays calm however many you star.

  • My suggestion: B in either case, plus F1 if you'll use two or three favourites every evening; F2 if you'd rather keep the home screen minimal or star many.
  • Favourites are kept on the TV (the Hue API doesn't expose the Hue app's own favourites). A starred scene that's deleted in the Hue app simply disappears.
  • Brightness applies to the whole room or zone (all its lights), like the room slider in the Hue app. The bar's dot doesn't change; the slider shows the current value when the list opens.
