Wallpapers per item

Each input or dock app can have its own wallpapers (./tv.sh wallpaper add). When it gets focus, the background fades over in 0.5 s to a random one of them; when focus moves to something without wallpapers, it fades back to the default. Click a frame, then use ◀ ▶ ▲ ▼. The PS5 has two pictures (your rooftop image and a mirrored crop as a stand-in for a second one), so pressing on and off it shows the random pick. The other items have none here.

P1 · Muted, like today

The same darkening and strong blur as the current wallpaper. Calm and always readable, but the art becomes mostly colour.

P2 · Light blur

Less dark, a soft 4 px blur: the picture is recognisable, the UI still sits on a calm background.

P3 · Sharp, with dark bands

The picture as it is, slightly darkened, with darker bands at the top and bottom so the clock, the bars and the dock stay readable. Shows the art best.

  • My suggestion: P2. The art reads as itself, and the glass panels and clock stay calm. P3 looks great with a clean picture but depends a lot on each image.
  • The glass panels always blur what's behind them, in every variant.
  • On the TV the images are shrunk once when Home starts, so focus changes cost nothing. P3 needs a larger copy than the 480×270 used today (a sharp picture can't be stored that small); 960×540 is fine on a 1080p screen.
