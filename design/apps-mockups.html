More apps & choosing home apps

Both build on layout D. The app list is what's actually installed on the TV (10 apps plus Settings). Plex, YouTube and SVT Play use their real banners; the others are placeholders, since the launcher will show each app's own banner. Click to focus, arrow keys to move; in S1 and S2 clicking also toggles an app on or off the home screen.

What "More apps" opens

M1 · Glass sheet

A frosted sheet slides up over the dimmed home screen with every app in a grid. Back slides it away. Simple, and the home screen stays visible as context.

M2 · The dock grows

No new screen: the pill stretches into one long row. Your home apps first, a thin divider, then everything else; ◀ ▶ scrolls. The most minimal, but slow to scan with many apps.

M3 · App drawer

A full screen of its own over a deeper-blurred wallpaper: "On Home" on top, all other apps below. Clearest overview, and it shows what's pinned without any extra UI.

Choosing which apps are on the home screen

S1 · Checkbox list with live preview

A "Home screen apps" page (reached from the end of the More apps view, or a long press on the dock). Check an app to add it; on a checked app ◀ ▶ moves it in the order. The mini home screen on the right updates as you go. Click the rows to try it.

S2 · Pin straight from the grid

Like the stock launcher: in the More apps grid, OK opens an app and a long press on OK adds or removes it from Home. A check badge marks the ones on Home. No separate settings page. Click tiles to toggle.

S3 · Edit mode on the dock

Long press a tile on the home screen: the tiles wobble, ◀ ▶ moves the focused one, OK removes it, and the + at the end opens a picker. Back finishes.

  • My suggestion: M3 for More apps, with S2's long press to pin/unpin there. S3's edit mode is a nice addition later for reordering; S1 is the most explicit if you'd rather have a settings page.
  • Finding apps: the launcher asks Android for every app with a TV launcher entry, so new apps from Play Store show up by themselves. Our own launcher is left out; Settings is added as a fixed entry.
  • Saving the choice: the home apps and their order are kept on the TV (SharedPreferences), so they survive restarts and app updates.
