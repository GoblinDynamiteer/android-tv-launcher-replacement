HDMI inputs on the home screen

Three takes on the top-right corner, each on the real layout D. The inputs are the ones the TV reports right now: PlayStation 5 (HDMI 1, named over HDMI-CEC) and Steam Machine (HDMI 2, your name from the Philips source settings). Click a frame, then use the arrow keys: ▲ from the dock goes up to the inputs, ▼ back down, Enter "switches".

I1 · Labelled pills

Two small glass pills with a gamepad icon and the name, always readable. Focus works like the dock: a white ring and a slight lift.

I2 · One input bar

Both inputs in a single glass bar, like a status strip, led by a gamepad icon. A dot shows whether the console is on (green) or in standby. The focused segment turns solid white. The first frame starts on the dock, the second with Steam Machine focused.

I3 · Icons, name on focus

The most minimal: round glass buttons with just the icon. The focused one widens to show its name. Calmest corner, but you have to focus a button to know which is which.

  • My suggestion: I1. It's readable from the sofa without focusing anything, matches the dock, and stays small. I2's status dots can be added to I1 later if you want them.
  • Which inputs show: HDMI ports with a CEC device (named by the device) or a name you gave them in the Philips settings, or with a cable that reports a connection. Empty HDMI 3 and 4 stay hidden; plug something in later and it appears by itself.
  • Status dots (I2): the PS5 reports on/standby over CEC. Whether the Steam Machine does depends on its CEC support; otherwise its dot would only mean "connected".
  • Switching: OK opens the input full screen in Philips' Live TV app (tested over adb: works). Home comes back here.
