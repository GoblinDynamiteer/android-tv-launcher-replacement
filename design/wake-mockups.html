Waking the Steam Machine

Selecting Steam Machine while it's asleep (grey dot) sends a wake packet; it takes about 12 s to come up (measured). Two ways to spend those seconds. Press "Select Steam Machine" under a strip to play it through. When it's already on (green dot), both just switch input as today.

W1 · Switch right away

Wake packet and input switch at once: the TV shows HDMI 2 with "no signal" (black) until the Steam Machine's picture arrives. Simplest; no change to the bar.

W2 · Wait on Home, then switch

You stay on the home screen: the dot pulses amber and the label says "waking…". As soon as HDMI 2 reports a signal (the launcher already listens for that), it switches by itself. If nothing happens within 45 s: a short "Steam Machine didn't wake" message. Back or picking something else cancels.

  • My suggestion: W2. No black "no signal" screen, you can see something is happening, and nothing switches if the wake fails.
  • The PS5 keeps working as today (it can't be woken this way); the same hook could later wake it over the network.
